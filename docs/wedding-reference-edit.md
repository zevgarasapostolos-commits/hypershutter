# The user's wedding edit (γαμοβάφτιση, 25 July): what HyperShutter learns from it

Read with `core/prproj.py` from a copy of the user's final Premiere project (v2). No client
names. These observations are the source for the wedding highlights and ροή builders.
The user's own instructions are:

- The highlights start with an intro that ends with titles (place and the couple's names).
  The user adds the titles himself; we leave a clean shot and a marker.
- Then comes a story of spontaneous moments and laughter from the getting-ready, a transition
  into the ceremony, the couple's photo shoot, and then the party.
- The app makes only the cuts, music and sound design. There is no grading and no effects.
  The output is XML.
- For now, use only the music and SFX the user already used. Baptisms always use the same song.

## Rules HyperShutter applies to weddings

1. **Highlights:**
   - teaser (best moments of the whole day, ~25 s) → title marker on a clean shot → place →
     details → getting ready (bride/groom intercut, live-sound moments) → church & arrival →
     ceremony → exit & wishes → couple shoot → (baptism → family with baby) → reception →
     party → final drone;
   - one song per part, crossfaded;
   - cuts on the beat; shot length ~1.6 s, ~1.15 s in the party.
2. **Film scenes:** the ceremony's key moments (vows, crowns, common cup, «Ησαΐα χόρευε»,
   immersion), with live sound and the projector SFX. The overlay is the user's.
3. **Sound design:** ambience beds under openings and drones; whooshes into the title and at
   section changes.
4. **Ροή:**
   - the ceremony keeps its own sound, with a soft music bed under it (camera −3 dB, music
     −6 to −11 dB; mic +3 dB, music −15 to −23 dB);
   - music ducks under interviews and speech;
   - dances use camera sound only.
5. **Nothing is thrown away:** every posed group and every party shot belongs somewhere.
